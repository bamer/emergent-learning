## H-109: Clarification R-Type horizontal scrolling: Player moves vertically (up/down) AND horizontally (forward/backward) WITHIN screen bounds, not just vertical only. Background scrolls LEFT (world moves left), player stays in designated play area. Classic R-Type mechanics: auto-scroll, player confined to box, enemies spawn from RIGHT side moving LEFT toward player.

**Confidence**: 0.98
**Source**: observation
**Created**: 2026-02-10

R-Type games like R-Type, Gradius have player confined to visible screen area (rectangular bounds), background auto-scrolls, enemies enter from right. Player can move in all 4 directions but is clamped to screen edges.
